## Animated stories for children aged 3 to 7

Fin the fox, Momo the bear and Tilly the duckling are three best friends in Little Wonder Woods. Every episode is one complete story in three chapters: a gentle adventure, a chant to sing along, questions for little viewers to answer out loud, and one small thing to learn (counting, colours, being brave, saying goodbye).

New episodes and Shorts on the [Three Wonder Tales YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ThreeWonderTales) every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## About the studio

Three Wonder Tales is produced by Little Wonder Woods Studio: original characters, stories, music and sound, made for kids. The videos are published on YouTube as "made for kids" content.
