Three Wonder Tales - Little Wonder Woods Studio - Last updated 26 October 2026

## What this covers

This policy describes how Little Wonder Woods Studio ("we") handles data in connection with the Three Wonder Tales YouTube channel and the "Little Wonder Woods Studio" application that we use to publish our own videos to that channel.

## The publishing application

Little Wonder Woods Studio is an internal desktop application used only by the channel owner. It connects to the YouTube Data API through Google sign-in in order to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, and add them to playlists on the Three Wonder Tales channel. It requests the YouTube permissions needed for this and nothing else.

The application stores the Google access token on the channel owner's own computer, in the operating system's credential store. It does not collect, store or share any data about viewers, and it does not read, collect or process any personal information from other YouTube users or Google accounts.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Viewers of our videos

Our videos are published on YouTube as "made for kids" content. We do not run our own website analytics, accounts, comments or forms, and we do not collect personal information from children or anyone else through this website. Viewing our videos is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and YouTube's terms.

## Revoking access

The channel owner can remove the application's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
